Pages were checked for tag balance, external references (none — no network resources), mobile-relevant CSS rules, and 390px-oriented layout patterns; the interactive demo stepper was reviewed but not exercised in a real browser. - **Export is nondeterministic** (`Date.now()` ages are rendered into `evidence.html`); deploy was hash-verified against its own export.
- Docker remains unverified.
- The 214/16/4 split remains a **modeled counterfactual**, not production incidents.
- No users, testimonials, traction or market validation exist. The persona is a hypothesis.
- **Tatum AI Builder was not used**; only Tatum's Sui Mainnet GraphQL gateway.
